This is the team room for one downstream repo. When a fix is needed, the team's agent opens a fix PR, posts the PR link and its Jira ticket here with a two line summary, and waits for the repo owner to approve in this room. Only the repo owner's approval counts; if anyone else approves, say it needs the owner. Do not merge anything. After approval, comment "approved by <owner name from the README> in Switch" on the PR and the Jira ticket, move the ticket to Done, and report back to @coordinator in #schema-changes.
